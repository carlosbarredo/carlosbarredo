## Research standard

> **Evidence before narrative. Costs before claims. Limitations beside results.**

I prefer bounded questions, explicit data contracts, reproducible code, and
results that remain useful when the original hypothesis fails. Negative results
are publishable results when the method and stopping decision are clear.
